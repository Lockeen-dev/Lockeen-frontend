About Lockeen — standard boilerplate

Lockeen is an AI-powered study platform that helps students learn faster and retain more. Founded in 2024, Lockeen combines adaptive flashcards, intelligent note summarization, and a conversational AI tutor to create a personalized learning experience for every student. With over 500,000 active users across 50+ countries and 10 million study sessions completed, Lockeen is one of the fastest-growing EdTech platforms globally. Lockeen is privately held and headquartered remotely, with team members across Europe and North America. For more information, visit lockeen.app.
